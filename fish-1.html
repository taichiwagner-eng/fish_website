Plastic

What is plastic?

Plastic is a material invented in 1907 that revolutionized the production industry, being mass-producible and cheaper to use.5 However, plastic was not designed to be reusable and is discarded after use. Most waste may be compiled into trash heaps, and some ends up in water sources.

How does plastic enter the ocean?

The ocean is the largest source of water, which also means plastic in water concentrates in the ocean. For example, trash littered on the beach directly enters the ocean, while trash from the riverbank can flow into the ocean. Floods can push debris and litter into the ocean as well.
